Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process

Every construction project is unique, but our structured cleaning process ensures every property is prepared for final inspection, occupancy, and project handover. We follow a systematic approach that delivers consistent, high-quality results while keeping your project on schedule.

1. Site Assessment

We evaluate the property, identify construction debris, dust accumulation, and determine the cleaning requirements for every area before work begins.

2. Debris Removal

Construction debris, packaging materials, protective coverings, and remaining waste are removed to prepare the site for detailed cleaning.

3. Detailed Dust Removal

Fine construction dust is removed from walls, ceilings, windows, fixtures, cabinets, vents, baseboards, and all accessible surfaces.

4. Deep Cleaning & Sanitization

Kitchens, bathrooms, floors, glass, doors, trim, fixtures, and high-touch surfaces are professionally cleaned and sanitized for a polished finish.

5. Final Quality Inspection

Every area is carefully inspected to verify quality, consistency, and readiness before project delivery, occupancy, or client handover.

6. Project Ready

Your property is delivered clean, organized, inspection-ready, and prepared for immediate occupancy with professional presentation standards.

Our structured post-construction cleaning process helps general contractors, builders, developers, renovation companies, and property owners deliver projects that are clean, safe, professionally presented, and ready for occupancy, inspections, or final client handover.

Frequently Asked Questions About Post-Construction Cleaning

Have questions about post-construction cleaning, construction cleanup, or post-renovation cleaning? Here are answers to common questions from homeowners, contractors, builders, property managers, and businesses.

What is included in post-construction cleaning?

Our post-construction cleaning includes fine construction dust removal, debris cleanup, detailed surface cleaning, window and glass cleaning, floor care, kitchen and bathroom detailing, fixture cleaning, baseboards, doors, frames, and final quality preparation. We work carefully through the property to help deliver a clean, organized, and welcoming space ready for its next stage.

Do you provide post-construction cleaning for commercial projects?

Yes. We provide post-construction cleaning for offices, retail spaces, commercial buildings, warehouses, medical facilities, and other commercial properties throughout Southeast New Brunswick. We work with the specific requirements of each project to help ensure the finished space is professionally presented and ready for inspection, handover, or occupancy.

Do you clean newly built homes and renovation projects?

Absolutely. We provide post-construction and post-renovation cleaning for newly built homes, residential renovations, condominiums, apartments, and other properties. Our goal is to take care of the final cleaning details so homeowners and project teams can move forward with greater confidence.

How much does post-construction cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the property, project scope, amount of construction dust and debris, condition of the space, and level of detail required. We provide customized, no-obligation estimates based on the specific requirements of each project rather than using a one-size-fits-all price.

How long does post-construction cleaning take?

Completion time depends on the size, condition, and complexity of the property. Smaller residential projects may require only a few hours, while larger commercial or multi-stage projects may require one or more days. We assess the project requirements and communicate a realistic timeframe before the work begins.

How soon can you clean after construction or renovation is finished?

We can coordinate post-construction or post-renovation cleaning once the appropriate construction work has been completed and the space is ready for final cleaning. Timing depends on the project schedule, site conditions, and access requirements. We work with project teams to help keep the final stage moving as smoothly as possible.

Do you remove fine construction dust?

Yes. Fine construction dust can settle throughout a property, including on floors, walls, baseboards, windows, fixtures, vents, doors, and other accessible surfaces. Our team uses detailed cleaning procedures to remove construction dust and help prepare the space for its final presentation.

Do you clean windows, frames, vents, fixtures, and hard-to-reach areas?

Yes. Post-construction cleaning can include detailed attention to interior windows, frames, doors, fixtures, vents, baseboards, and other accessible areas where dust and construction residue can accumulate. Specific high or hard-to-reach areas can be discussed during the project assessment.

Can you work around our construction or project schedule?

Yes. We understand that the final cleaning is often connected to inspections, handovers, move-in dates, and project deadlines. When scheduling and site conditions allow, we coordinate our service around the project's timeline to help keep the final stage organized and on track.

Will the property be ready for inspection, handover, or occupancy?

Our goal is to leave the property clean, organized, and professionally presented for its next stage. Depending on the project scope, this may include final dust removal, surface detailing, floor care, windows, kitchens, bathrooms, fixtures, and a final quality inspection. We focus on the details that help your completed work make the right final impression.

Do you provide the cleaning equipment and supplies?

Yes. We can provide the professional cleaning products, equipment, and supplies required for the agreed scope of work. Project-specific requirements, surface considerations, and cleaning priorities can be discussed during the assessment so the approach is appropriate for the property.

Do you provide post-construction cleaning services near me?

Yes. We provide post-construction cleaning, construction cleanup, and post-renovation cleaning throughout Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac, Salisbury, Memramcook, and surrounding communities across Southeast New Brunswick. We aim to be a reliable partner during the final stage of your project, helping you move from completed construction to a clean and professionally presented space.
