Frequently Asked Questions About Commercial Cleaning Services

Have questions about commercial cleaning, office cleaning, janitorial services, or ongoing facility support? Here are answers to some of the questions businesses ask before choosing a professional cleaning partner.

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Moncton?

Commercial cleaning costs depend on the size of your facility, cleaning frequency, service requirements, and operational complexity. We provide customized quotes based on your specific facility and needs, so you receive a service plan that makes sense for your business rather than a one-size-fits-all solution.

How often should a business have its facility professionally cleaned?

It depends on your facility, daily traffic, type of business, and operational needs. Some businesses require daily service, while others may benefit from weekly, bi-weekly, or customized schedules. We help determine a practical cleaning frequency that keeps your facility consistently clean, welcoming, and well cared for.

Can commercial cleaning be scheduled outside business hours?

Yes. When scheduling and access requirements allow, we can coordinate cleaning around your operating hours to minimize disruption to employees, customers, and daily operations. Our goal is to make professional cleaning easier to manage, not another interruption to your business.

Can you customize a commercial cleaning program for our business?

Absolutely. Every facility is different. We consider your building size, layout, traffic, priorities, operating hours, and service expectations when developing a cleaning program. Our goal is to provide a reliable solution built around how your business actually operates.

Do you clean offices and corporate buildings?

Yes. We provide professional office and corporate cleaning services for businesses throughout Greater Moncton and surrounding communities. We focus on the details that help your workplace remain clean, organized, professional, and ready for employees, clients, and visitors.

Do you provide janitorial services for commercial facilities?

Yes. Our janitorial programs are designed for businesses that need dependable ongoing cleaning and sanitation. We work with you to establish a consistent service routine that supports the condition and day-to-day operation of your facility.

Do you serve warehouses and industrial facilities?

Yes. We provide commercial cleaning solutions for warehouses, industrial facilities, storage environments, logistics operations, and other commercial spaces. We take the time to understand the environment and its requirements so the cleaning approach supports both safety and operational needs.

Do you provide the cleaning products, equipment, and supplies?

Yes. We can provide the professional cleaning products, equipment, and supplies required for the agreed scope of service. During the assessment, we can discuss specific facility requirements, preferences, and any products or procedures that need special consideration.

How do you make sure the cleaning service stays consistent?

Consistency starts with clear expectations, structured cleaning procedures, attention to detail, and reliable communication. We approach every facility with accountability and professional standards because our responsibility does not end when the cleaning is finished. We want you to have confidence that your space is being properly cared for.

How do you protect our building, keys, and access information?

We understand that allowing a cleaning team into your facility requires trust. Access instructions, keys, entry procedures, and operational information are handled responsibly according to the requirements agreed upon with each client. Professionalism, discretion, and accountability are an important part of how we work.

Are your cleaning staff trained in workplace safety?

Yes. Our team follows workplace safety practices including WHMIS, IPAC, Bloodborne Awareness, and appropriate PPE protocols. We believe professional service also means taking responsibility for the safety of our team, your employees, your customers, and the environments where we work.

Do you provide commercial cleaning services near me?

Yes. We provide commercial cleaning, office cleaning, and janitorial services throughout Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac, and surrounding communities across Southeast New Brunswick. We are committed to providing dependable service and becoming a trusted partner for the businesses and facilities we serve.
