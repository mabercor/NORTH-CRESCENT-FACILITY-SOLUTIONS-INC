Frequently Asked Questions About Janitorial Services in Moncton

Have questions about recurring janitorial services, facility cleaning, scheduling, or ongoing maintenance? Here are answers to common questions businesses ask before choosing a professional janitorial partner.

How much do janitorial services cost for an office in Moncton?

Janitorial service costs depend on facility size, cleaning frequency, occupancy levels, service requirements, and operational complexity. We provide customized cleaning programs based on your facility's specific needs, helping you choose a reliable service without paying for unnecessary work.

How often should a commercial facility be professionally cleaned?

Cleaning frequency depends on foot traffic, facility usage, employee count, industry requirements, and the type of environment. Some facilities require daily service, while others may benefit from several visits per week or a customized schedule. We help determine a practical routine that keeps your facility consistently clean, safe, and welcoming.

Can janitorial services be scheduled after business hours?

Yes. When scheduling and access requirements allow, we can coordinate janitorial services during evenings, overnight periods, weekends, or other off-peak hours. Our goal is to provide dependable cleaning while minimizing disruption to your employees, customers, and daily operations.

What is included in a commercial janitorial program?

A janitorial program can include routine cleaning, restroom sanitation, waste removal, floor care, high-touch surface cleaning, common-area cleaning, supply monitoring, quality inspections, and scheduled additional services based on your facility's needs. We build the program around what your business actually requires.

Can you customize a janitorial program for our facility?

Absolutely. Every facility operates differently. We consider your building size, layout, traffic, operating hours, priority areas, cleaning frequency, and service expectations when creating a janitorial program. Our goal is to build a reliable routine around your operation, not force your business into a standard package.

How do I know if my current cleaning company is doing a good job?

A professional janitorial provider should deliver consistent results, follow clear cleaning procedures, maintain quality standards, and communicate when issues require attention. If the quality changes from visit to visit or important areas are repeatedly overlooked, it may be time to review the cleaning process and expectations.

What is the difference between janitorial services and commercial cleaning?

Commercial cleaning can refer to specific cleaning services or projects, while janitorial services typically focus on ongoing, recurring facility care. A janitorial program is designed to maintain cleanliness and consistency over time, helping businesses stay focused on their operations instead of managing cleaning concerns themselves.

Do you provide the cleaning products, equipment, and supplies?

Yes. We can provide the professional cleaning products, equipment, and supplies required for the agreed scope of service. Facility- specific requirements and product preferences can be discussed during the assessment so the cleaning approach is appropriate for your environment.

How do you maintain consistent janitorial cleaning quality?

Consistency starts with clear expectations, structured cleaning procedures, attention to detail, and reliable communication. We believe a janitorial service should provide dependable results from visit to visit, giving facility managers confidence that their space is being properly cared for.

How do you handle keys, building access, and security?

We understand that ongoing janitorial service often requires access to your facility outside regular business hours. Access instructions, keys, entry procedures, and other operational information are handled responsibly according to the requirements agreed upon with each client. Professionalism, discretion, and accountability are essential to how we work.

Can our janitorial service be adjusted as our facility needs change?

Yes. Business needs can change with occupancy, seasons, renovations, staffing, events, or changes in facility use. We can review your cleaning requirements and adjust the service scope or frequency when appropriate, helping your cleaning program continue to support the way your facility operates.

Do you provide janitorial services near me?

Yes. We provide janitorial and recurring facility cleaning services throughout Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac, and surrounding communities across Southeast New Brunswick. Our goal is to become a dependable cleaning partner that businesses can trust to care for their facilities consistently.

Is your janitorial service covered by a satisfaction guarantee?

Yes. Every janitorial service from North Crescent Facility Solutions in Moncton, Dieppe and Riverview includes our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. If something isn't right, let us know by text message, WhatsApp or email within 24 hours and we'll come back to fix it at no extra cost. See the guarantee conditions in our Terms & Conditions.
