Frequently Asked Questions About Our Cleaning Services

Have questions about professional cleaning services? Here are some of the questions homeowners, tenants, property owners, and businesses ask before choosing the right cleaning solution for their space.

How much do cleaning services cost in Moncton?

Cleaning service pricing depends on the size of the property, condition of the space, type of service, frequency, and level of detail required. North Crescent Facility Solutions provides customized residential and commercial cleaning quotes based on the actual needs of each property.

What cleaning services does North Crescent Facility Solutions offer?

We provide residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, office cleaning, janitorial services, deep cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, Airbnb and short-term rental cleaning, and post-construction cleaning. We also provide selected property support services to help clients keep their spaces properly cared for.

Do you offer one-time and recurring cleaning services?

Yes. We offer both one-time and recurring cleaning services. Depending on your needs, this can include a single deep cleaning, move-in or move-out cleaning, post-construction cleaning, or an ongoing cleaning program designed around your property and schedule.

How do I know which cleaning service is right for my property?

It depends on what your space needs. Regular cleaning is designed for ongoing maintenance, while deep cleaning provides more detailed attention to areas that need a reset. Moving, renovating, managing an Airbnb, or operating a commercial facility may require a more specialized service. We can help you determine the right approach for your property.

Can I customize a cleaning service for my home or business?

Absolutely. Every property is different, and not every client needs the same level of service. We can discuss your property size, priorities, frequency, specific areas, and additional requirements to create a cleaning plan that makes sense for your space.

How often should my home or business be professionally cleaned?

The ideal frequency depends on the type of property, traffic, occupancy, daily activity, and level of use. Some spaces benefit from weekly or bi-weekly cleaning, while commercial facilities may require several visits per week. We can help establish a schedule that keeps your space consistently cared for.

Can I book same-day cleaning in Moncton?

Same-day and short-notice cleaning may be available depending on our schedule and the scope of the service. If you have an urgent cleaning need, contact us as soon as possible and we will determine whether we can accommodate your request.

Do you provide deep cleaning services in Moncton?

Yes. Our deep cleaning services provide detailed attention to accumulated dirt, dust, grease, buildup, baseboards, doors, fixtures, and commonly overlooked areas. Deep cleaning can be a valuable reset before beginning a recurring cleaning routine.

Can deep cleaning remove grease, dirt, and buildup?

Deep cleaning is designed to address accumulated dirt, grease, dust, and buildup that may require more detailed attention than routine cleaning. The exact level of cleaning depends on the condition and surfaces within the property.

Do you provide move-in and move-out cleaning in Moncton?

Yes. Our move-in and move-out cleaning services help prepare homes, apartments, condos, and rental properties for new occupants, inspections, property transitions, or final presentation. The service can be adjusted according to the property's condition and requirements.

Do you provide Airbnb and short-term rental cleaning?

Yes. We provide Airbnb and short-term rental turnover cleaning designed to help prepare properties for incoming guests. Our focus is on consistent presentation, detailed cleaning, and helping property owners and hosts maintain a guest-ready space.

Do you provide post-construction and renovation cleaning?

Yes. We provide post-construction and post-renovation cleaning for residential and commercial projects. Services can include detailed dust removal, surface cleaning, floor care, fixtures, windows, and final preparation for inspection, handover, or occupancy.

Do you provide office and commercial cleaning services?

Yes. We provide professional cleaning solutions for offices, commercial spaces, retail environments, facilities, and other business properties. Services can be structured around recurring cleaning, deep cleaning, janitorial care, or specific project requirements.

Can commercial cleaning be scheduled after business hours?

Yes. Depending on the service and schedule, commercial cleaning can be arranged before or after regular business hours to help minimize disruption to employees, customers, and daily operations.

What is the difference between commercial cleaning and janitorial services?

Commercial cleaning can refer to specific cleaning services or projects, while janitorial services are generally structured around ongoing facility care and maintenance. We can help determine which type of service, or combination of services, best supports your business and facility.

Are your cleaning staff trained in safe cleaning practices?

Our cleaning operations follow structured procedures focused on professional standards, workplace safety, proper cleaning methods, attention to detail, and responsible handling of cleaning tasks. We believe professionalism includes taking care of the people, property, and spaces entrusted to us.

Do you provide cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. We provide the cleaning products and equipment required for the agreed service. If your property has specific surface requirements, product preferences, or special instructions, these can be discussed when planning your cleaning service.

What areas does North Crescent Facility Solutions serve?

We provide professional cleaning and facility support services throughout Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac, and surrounding communities in Southeast New Brunswick. If you are located outside our primary service area, contact us to discuss your location and cleaning requirements.
