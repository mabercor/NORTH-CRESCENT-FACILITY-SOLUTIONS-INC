Do you offer seasonal snow removal contracts?

Yes. We offer seasonal snow removal plans designed to help homeowners organize their winter maintenance in advance. Our seasonal service period runs from November 15 through April 15, with plan features and service support determined by the package selected.

Is salt or ice treatment included in snow removal service?

Ice treatment is included in our Premium seasonal plan. Essential and Standard customers can request ice treatment separately at an additional cost, subject to availability. How quickly will my driveway be cleared after a snowfall?

Service timing depends on snowfall conditions, storm severity, route scheduling, and your selected seasonal plan. Our plans provide different levels of service priority, including priority support during significant storms. Specific arrival times are not guaranteed unless expressly agreed in writing.

What happens during heavy snowfall or freezing rain?

Heavy snowfall and freezing rain can create changing conditions that require additional attention. Our seasonal plans offer different levels of storm priority and winter support. Ice treatment is included with Premium, while additional treatment for other plans may be requested separately. Service remains subject to weather and operating conditions.

What is the difference between Essential, Standard, and Premium?

Essential provides core seasonal snow clearing for designated access areas. Premium includes Standard features, ice treatment, enhanced winter communication, and additional priority support. Review our seasonal plans for the complete list of inclusions.
