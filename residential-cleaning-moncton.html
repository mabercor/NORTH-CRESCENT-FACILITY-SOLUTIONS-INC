Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Cleaning

Have questions about residential cleaning, deep cleaning, recurring house cleaning, or professional home care? Here are answers to some of the questions homeowners ask before choosing a cleaning service.

How much does residential cleaning cost in Moncton?

Pricing depends on the size of your home, the type of cleaning required, the condition of the space, and the frequency of service. We provide customized quotes based on your home's specific needs, so you receive a cleaning solution that fits your home rather than a one-size-fits-all service.

Do you provide recurring house cleaning services?

Yes. We offer weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, and customized residential cleaning schedules designed around your home's needs and your preferred routine. Our goal is to provide dependable service that helps you enjoy a consistently clean and comfortable home without adding another responsibility to your schedule.

How often should I have my home professionally cleaned?

It depends on your home's size, daily activity, lifestyle, and cleaning priorities. Some homes benefit from weekly service, while others may be better suited to bi-weekly or monthly cleaning. We can help you choose a practical schedule based on what works best for your home and your routine.

What's the difference between regular cleaning and deep cleaning?

Regular cleaning focuses on maintaining the cleanliness and comfort of your home on an ongoing basis. Deep cleaning provides additional attention to areas and surfaces that may require more detailed care. We can recommend the appropriate service based on the condition and priorities of your home.

Do I need a deep cleaning before starting recurring service?

Not necessarily. Every home is different. If your home needs additional attention before beginning a regular cleaning routine, we can recommend a deep cleaning first. Our goal is to establish a practical starting point and then help you maintain your home consistently.

Do you provide move-in and move-out cleaning?

Yes. We provide professional move-in and move-out cleaning to help prepare a home for a new occupant or leave a property clean and ready for its next stage. We focus on the details that help make the transition easier and less stressful.

Do you provide residential sanitization services?

Yes. Our residential cleaning services can include professional cleaning and sanitization practices based on the needs of your home. We focus on careful, responsible service designed to help maintain a clean, comfortable, and welcoming living environment.

Do you bring your own cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. We can provide the professional cleaning products, equipment, and supplies required for the agreed scope of service. If you have specific product preferences or household considerations, we can discuss them during the consultation so the service is appropriate for your home.

Do I need to be home while you clean?

Not necessarily. Cleaning arrangements can be coordinated around your schedule and access preferences. Whether you are home or away, we approach every property with professionalism, respect, and care, because we understand that we are being trusted with your home.

Are your residential cleaning staff trained?

Yes. Our team follows professional cleaning and safety practices, including WHMIS, IPAC, Bloodborne Pathogens awareness, and appropriate PPE protocols. We believe professional cleaning also means treating your home, your belongings, and your family with care and respect.

Why should I hire a professional residential cleaner?

Professional cleaning can help reduce the time and stress involved in maintaining your home, giving you more time for your family, your loved ones, your work, and the things that matter most. We are here to make home care easier while providing the professionalism and attention to detail your home deserves.

Do you provide residential cleaning services near me?

Yes. We provide residential cleaning services throughout Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac, Pointe-du-Chêne, Cap-Pelé, Cocagne, Grande-Digue, and surrounding communities throughout Southeast New Brunswick. Our goal is to provide dependable, thoughtful service that helps homeowners feel confident their home is properly cared for.

Is your residential cleaning service covered by a satisfaction guarantee?

Yes. Every residential cleaning service from North Crescent Facility Solutions in Moncton, Dieppe and Riverview includes our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. If something isn't right, let us know by text message, WhatsApp or email within 24 hours and we'll come back to fix it at no extra cost. See the guarantee conditions in our Terms & Conditions.
