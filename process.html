Step by step

How Our Professional Cleaning Process Works in Moncton

Our workflow is designed to maintain control, consistency, and high-performance standards across every project — no shortcuts, no variability.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Request & Assessment

    Tell us about your property through our booking page, Concierge or a quick call. We review your space, priorities, schedule and any special requirements.

    Clear scope of work
  2. 2

    Step 2: Customized Plan

    We design a structured cleaning plan for your property: the areas, the frequency, the products and the timing that fit how you operate.

    Tailored plan & quote
  3. 3

    Step 3: Professional Execution

    Our own trained, background-checked team arrives prepared with professional equipment and follows the plan area by area.

    Detailed, consistent cleaning
  4. 4

    Step 4: Quality Control

    Every area is inspected against the plan. Our technology platform records the work so you receive real-time service reports.

    Real-time service report
  5. 5

    Step 5: Client Approval

    We confirm your satisfaction before closing the job, and fine-tune the plan for the next visit if anything needs adjusting.

    Your sign-off & follow-up
