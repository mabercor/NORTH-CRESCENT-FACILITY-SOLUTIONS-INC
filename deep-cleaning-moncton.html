Our Deep Cleaning Process

Every deep cleaning service begins with understanding the condition of the property and ends with a detailed review of the completed work. Our structured process helps ensure that important areas receive the attention they require.

1. Property Assessment

We review the property, identify areas requiring additional attention, and determine the cleaning priorities and overall scope of the service.

2. Service Preparation

Before cleaning begins, we organize the required tasks and prepare the service approach based on the property's condition, layout, and agreed cleaning requirements.

3. Detailed Deep Cleaning

Our team works through the agreed areas systematically, focusing on accumulated dirt, dust, buildup, high-touch surfaces, floors, kitchens, bathrooms, and other priority areas.

4. Detail & Finishing Work

Accessible surfaces, edges, fixtures, doors, baseboards, and other commonly overlooked areas receive additional attention where required.

5. Quality Inspection

Completed areas are reviewed to verify that the agreed cleaning scope has been addressed and that the property meets our service expectations.

6. Clean & Refreshed Property

Once the service is complete, the property is left cleaner, refreshed, organized, and ready for normal use or the next stage of occupancy.

Frequently Asked Questions About Deep Cleaning Services

Have questions about professional deep cleaning? These are some of the most common questions homeowners, tenants, property owners, and businesses ask before booking a deep cleaning service.

What is included in a deep cleaning service?

Deep cleaning provides detailed attention to kitchens, bathrooms, floors, baseboards, doors, frames, fixtures, accessible surfaces, high-touch areas, and other parts of a property that may need more attention than routine cleaning. The goal is to give your space a more thorough reset and restore a clean, comfortable feeling.

What is the difference between regular cleaning and deep cleaning?

Regular cleaning is designed to maintain a property on an ongoing basis, while deep cleaning focuses on accumulated dirt, dust, buildup, and commonly overlooked areas. Deep cleaning is ideal when a space needs more detailed attention before returning to a regular cleaning routine.

When should I book a deep cleaning for my home?

A deep cleaning can be helpful when your home needs a fresh start, after a busy period, before guests arrive, during seasonal changes, or when routine cleaning is no longer enough. It can also be a great way to reset your home before beginning recurring cleaning services.

How much does deep cleaning cost in Moncton?

Deep cleaning pricing depends on factors such as the size and condition of the property, the areas requiring attention, and the level of detail needed. We assess each space individually so the service can be planned around your actual cleaning needs rather than using a one-size-fits-all approach.

How long does a deep cleaning take?

The time required depends on the size, condition, layout, and amount of detail involved. A home that needs significant buildup removed may require more time than a property that has been regularly maintained. We consider the condition of the space when planning the service.

Do you clean baseboards, doors, frames, and hard-to-reach areas?

Yes. Deep cleaning allows us to give additional attention to areas that are commonly overlooked during routine cleaning, including baseboards, doors, frames, corners, fixtures, and accessible hard-to-reach areas.

Do you clean behind and under appliances?

Where safely accessible, we can provide additional attention around and behind appliances as part of a deep cleaning service. Specific tasks can be discussed when planning your service so the cleaning can match the condition and needs of your property.

Can you deep clean a home that has been neglected or needs extra attention?

Yes. We understand that every home goes through different seasons of life, and there is no judgment. If a property needs extra care because of accumulated dirt, dust, buildup, or simply a long period without detailed cleaning, we can focus on bringing the space back to a more comfortable and cared-for condition.

Do you bring your own cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. We arrive prepared with the cleaning products and equipment needed for the agreed service. If you have specific product preferences, surfaces that require special care, or areas with particular instructions, let us know when scheduling.

Can I request specific areas or additional cleaning tasks?

Yes. Deep cleaning can be tailored to the condition of your property and the areas that matter most to you. During the assessment, you can identify specific priorities or additional tasks so we can plan the service accordingly.

Can deep cleaning be used before moving, selling, or after renovations?

Absolutely. Deep cleaning can help prepare a property before moving in, after moving out, before listing a home for sale, or after renovations when additional cleaning is needed. For heavier construction-related dust and debris, we can also determine whether post-construction cleaning is the more appropriate service.

Do you provide deep cleaning services near me?

Yes. North Crescent Facility Solutions provides professional deep cleaning services throughout Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac, and surrounding communities in Southeast New Brunswick. Contact us to discuss your property and determine the right level of service for your needs.
